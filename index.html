Thomas Van Den Driessche, Belgian documentary photographer

Thomas Van Den Driessche is a Belgian documentary photographer based in Waterloo, near Brussels. His long-form stories look at contemporary India and its rapid industrialisation, at Belgium's rituals and post-industrial towns, and at Eastern Europe's unrecognised states. He also makes portraits and editorial commissions for Le Monde, Monocle, Libération and The New York Times.
